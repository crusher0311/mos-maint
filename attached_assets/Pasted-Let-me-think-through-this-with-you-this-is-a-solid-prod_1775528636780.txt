Let me think through this with you — this is a solid product opportunity sitting right at the intersection of your existing platform and advisor workflow.
The Core Problem You’re Solving
Service advisors lose time and accuracy at three moments:
	1.	Translating a diagnosis into a written estimate (wording, labor ops, pricing)
	2.	Sourcing the right parts (wrong part number, wrong application, wrong supplier)
	3.	Connecting those two things — the estimate and the parts quote — into something coherent fast

Feature Concept: “Estimate Assist” inside Detect Dog
Here’s how it could work as a layered feature:
Layer 1 — Smart Job Builder
	∙	Advisor selects (or types) the repair job — e.g., “front brake service”
	∙	Detect Dog, already knowing the VIN/year/make/model from the sticker/VHI data, auto-populates:
	∙	Standard labor description (professional, customer-friendly language)
	∙	Estimated flat-rate labor hours (pulling from a labor guide or your own baseline)
	∙	Flagged related jobs to upsell (e.g., brake fluid if doing brakes)
Layer 2 — PartsTech Integration
	∙	Once the job is built, a “Source Parts” button fires a pre-filled PartsTech query using the decoded VIN data you already have
	∙	No re-keying year/make/model — it flows through
	∙	Results surface directly in the estimate builder with price/availability
	∙	Advisor picks the part, it locks into the estimate line item
Layer 3 — AI-Assisted Estimate Language
	∙	Claude (or your own prompt layer) rewrites the estimate line in customer-friendly language automatically
	∙	Advisor can toggle between “technical” and “customer-facing” language
	∙	Catches missing information before the estimate goes out (e.g., “you added labor but no part”)

Why This Works in Your Stack
You already have the hardest part: VIN-decoded vehicle data tied to a customer record. That’s what makes PartsTech lookups accurate — wrong VIN application is the #1 source of bad parts orders. Most shops are re-keying that data every time. You’d eliminate that entirely.
The MOS sticker/VHI is already the first touchpoint. Estimate Assist becomes the second touchpoint — you’re not just reminding them the car exists, you’re helping them act on it.

Competitive Angle vs. Detect Auto
If Detect Auto doesn’t have deep PartsTech integration or an AI estimate layer, this is a meaningful differentiation — especially for independent shops and small MSOs who don’t have dedicated parts managers. You’re giving a 2-person shop the workflow efficiency of a 5-person operation.

Questions Worth Answering Before Building
	1.	Does PartsTech have a public API you can hit, or would this require a partnership/reseller arrangement? Worth checking their developer docs — some integrations go through a distributor layer.
	2.	What’s your current data model for vehicle records? If VIN is already stored and decoded, Layer 2 is mostly plumbing.
	3.	Is the estimate written inside MOS Tools, or does it go into their shop management system (SMS)? If it’s SMS-native (Mitchell, Tekmetric, etc.), you may want this to be a copy-paste assist rather than a full builder.
