This is purely a service-level OOM. They will not be graceful shutdowns, as your service is exiting outside of when we expect it to.

I'm not sure why it would have just started, but I see it going back as far as April:


I'm unsure why the TLS handshake would differ, since TLS for clients is terminated in Cloudflare. It is possible something has changed here without our knowledge, but I think you should first look into why you are hitting Node OOMs.

I see you set --max-old-space-size=3072 but you are running a 16GB instance. That is a lot of memory left unused. Try setting it to --max-old-space-size=12288 (maybe even higher depending on how much non-heap memory your service uses). Though if your service is leaking memory somewhere, this may just delay the crash.

Note, I wouldn't recommend setting max-old-space-size in a NODE_OPTIONS env var. This means it applies to both build and runtime environments, which have different amounts of memory. I would recommend setting it specifically in your build and start commands.
