When multiple inspections are attached to a Tekmetric repair order, Detect Dog only autofills the inspection positioned at the bottom of the inspections page.

I tested this several ways:

1. When the MPI was the only inspection on the RO, Detect Dog autofilled it correctly.
2. When both the MPI and internal inspection were attached and the MPI was positioned at the bottom, Detect Dog autofilled only the MPI.
3. When I changed the order and placed the internal inspection at the bottom, Detect Dog autofilled only the internal inspection.

This appears to show that Detect Dog is targeting the last inspection displayed on the page rather than a specific inspection template.

Expected behavior:
Detect Dog should either autofill the selected inspection, autofill the MPI specifically, or provide an option to choose which inspection receives the information.

Current workaround:
I must keep the MPI positioned at the bottom of the inspection page before using autofill.
