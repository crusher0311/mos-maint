---
name: Enterprise history sharing boundary
description: Why cross-location evidence must remain separate from local plan and write authority.
---

Treat enterprise sharing as a read permission, never as an extension of current-shop provider write authority. Keep signed/public and partner delivery local-only until those credentials gain an explicit redistribution grant.

**Why:** A VIN can exist in unrelated tenants, while signed reports and partner credentials authorize a local shop rather than an employee's enterprise assignments. Reusing employee sharing scope there would disclose sibling records.

**How to apply:** Recheck current membership, explicit assignments, sharing policy and per-location entitlements for every shared read. If future work puts shared evidence into plan/analysis/prefetch caches, introduce scope and source-revision validation first; the separate uncached evidence section deliberately avoids those persisted caches.

The user wants workflow vehicle/job cards to open combined vehicle history across enterprise locations.

**Why:** Dispatchers need prior vehicle work available from the scheduling board rather than switching screens.

**How to apply:** Reuse the authorized VIN-scoped history view, retain source-location attribution and coverage caveats, and do not interpret “all enterprise levels” as permission to bypass location access or sharing policy.
