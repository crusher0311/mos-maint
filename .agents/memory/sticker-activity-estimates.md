---
name: Sticker activity estimates
description: Product boundaries for label consumption and inactive-shop outreach.
---

Roll capacity varies by shop. Roll tracking must be opt-in, initialized explicitly,
and described as a single shop-level estimate, never inferred from lifetime totals.
Browser/PDF handoffs cannot confirm physical label consumption.

**Why:** The user confirmed varying roll sizes and requested a seven-day in-app
follow-up flag rather than email. Historical duplicate generations cannot be
repaired reliably by halving counts.

**How to apply:** Keep physical-print claims separate from recorded activity,
preserve old history, and require separate approval for production writes,
historical repair, automatic ordering, or notification expansion.
