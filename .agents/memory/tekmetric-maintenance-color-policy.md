---
name: Tekmetric overdue maintenance color policy
description: Explicit opt-in scope and the distinction between maintenance timing and inspection condition.
---
Generic overdue maintenance must remain red by default for existing and new shops. Yellow is a deliberate per-location opt-in, not a recommended default or an enterprise distribution policy.

**Why:** The user explicitly required preserving current guidelines unless a shop chooses otherwise. Inspection color is not permission to change VHI overdue calculations or to weaken prior technician findings.

**How to apply:** Keep maintenance timing distinct from condition ratings in any future presentation. Do not recolor saved inspection lines when this preference changes, and do not extend it to other providers without approval.
