---
name: Arrival photo association policy
description: Conservative evidence boundary for linking intake photos to staff findings.
---
Arrival-photo associations are internal contextual references, not inspection-photo evidence or customer-release selections. Require a saved evaluation before linking; keep required inspection photos, measurements and notes independent.

**Why:** An intake photo does not establish that a technician performed the checklist procedure. Reuse must not silently complete required inspection evidence.

**How to apply:** Any future request to count arrival photos toward requirements or include them in customer reports needs an explicit policy decision rather than treating the association as an uploaded inspection photo.
