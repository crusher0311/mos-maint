The user approved consolidating production DVI onto workflow visit-bound staff
evidence, not enabling the held VIN-keyed intermediate engine or replacing it
with the tire-only demo.

**Why:** Similar-looking inspection interfaces have different identity and
authorization contracts. Production capability is not live activation approval.

**How to apply:** Keep design artifacts read-only, require explicit visit
selection, preserve legacy receipts/links and do not retire old capabilities
until visit-bound parity is demonstrated. Video extraction remains separate
work; no consolidation implementation authorizes live stores, probes or writes.

Legacy provider capability must remain usable outside the approved pilot until
the replacement has parity and retirement approval; a workflow binding alone
does not authorize disabling it. Reject misrepresented visit evidence separately.
**Why:** The visit exporter has narrower enrollment than the incumbent writer.

Standalone preview retirement is a separate workstream. A combined project diff
may include that retirement even though DVI consolidation edits must not alter
the standalone artifacts.
**Why:** Concurrent work can merge independently; do not attribute every artifact
change in a combined diff to consolidation or undo another workstream.
