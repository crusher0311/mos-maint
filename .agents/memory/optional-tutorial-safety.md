---
name: Optional tutorial safety
description: Production help must remain opt-in and cannot activate business controls.
---
Tutorials belong to the production MOS dashboard and Detect Dog extension, not the standalone workflow or tire demos. Treat unknown account age as existing; never infer a new customer from empty preferences.

**Why:** The user explicitly approved short, optional help while protecting experienced customers from forced onboarding and forbidding customer-data, send, and print actions during walkthroughs.

**How to apply:** Guide steps explain existing controls without clicking them or fetching their data. Missing targets get a prerequisite explanation, not automatic navigation or refresh. Persistent choices need a server-authenticated person and a matching expected-person precondition so stale tabs cannot save another account's choices. Keep Basic sessions session-only and preserve extension write policy.
