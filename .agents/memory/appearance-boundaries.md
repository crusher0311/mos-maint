---
name: Appearance scope
description: Local appearance versus shop branding and physical output.
---
The website and workflow preview may follow System or have separate local choices. Appearance must not become shop configuration, reset with workflow data, or affect the unrelated tire demo, extension, emails, or physical output.

**Why:** The user explicitly requested optional screen dark mode without replacing branding or changing integration behavior. Shared logo inheritance and intentional overrides remain authoritative.

**How to apply:** Keep identity colors and screen surfaces separate; treat label, QR and document rendering as output contracts, not screen theme defaults. Verify against offline fixtures rather than starting shared-store-backed services.
