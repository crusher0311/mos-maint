---
name: Invoice repair hold authority
description: User-approved authoritative hold registry and its coverage-review boundary.
---
Use a dedicated registry for financial and ownership holds. Require reviewed
coverage before treating an empty registry as evidence that a repair is unheld.

**Why:** The user approved this approach after the existing preflights were
found to explicitly disclaim a complete financial/ownership-hold inventory.
Limited retained reviews must not silently become universal clearance.

**How to apply:** Keep coverage review separate from hold resolution and
action-specific execution approval. Missing or stale coverage blocks repairs.
Preparing the registry does not authorize production installation, populating
it, approving coverage, or executing repairs.
